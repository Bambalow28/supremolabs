ESYNC Privacy Policy

Effective 6 October 2026 · Supremo Labs

ESYNC is an iOS app for electric-vehicle owners to track charging costs, service and trips. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and your choices.

What we collect

  • Account: your Sign in with Apple identifier, and the email or name Apple shares if you allow it.
  • Data you enter: charge sessions, prices, notes, service items, vehicle details, forum posts and settings.
  • Location: only if you choose "use my location" to pin a charger. Not collected in the background.
  • Tesla data (only if you connect your Tesla): vehicle name and model, odometer, battery and charge state, drive state and location, used to log trips and charge sessions.

How we use it

Only to run the features you see in the app: your charge history and costs, trips, service reminders and boards. We do not sell your data, share it for advertising, or use it to profile you.

Connecting your Tesla

Connecting is optional and uses Tesla's official sign-in. We never see your Tesla password. We request read-only access to vehicle data; ESYNC cannot lock, unlock, start or otherwise control your car. You can disconnect in the app at any time, or revoke access at accounts.tesla.com. On disconnect we delete the stored Tesla tokens.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, Toronto region) under your account. Private data is readable only by you. Board posts are public to other ESYNC users. Our service providers are Apple (sign-in, distribution), Google Firebase (storage, authentication) and Tesla (only when you connect).

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. Email us to delete your account and all its data, or to get a copy of it.

Children

ESYNC is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and note significant changes in the app.
