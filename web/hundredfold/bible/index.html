The night Dad died, the bills started arriving in your name: a $60,000 estate that isn't yours. Hundredfold Staffing sends you to a one-night temp job in a building that wants you dead. Clock out alive and the pay is real. Don't, and it's $0 — and the shift still costs you a day toward rent.

LOOP 01 · MINUTESThe shift

Walk a generated floor, solve every terminal, dodge the hunter, reach the exit. Pay banks only on clock-out.

LOOP 02 · DAYSThe money

Rent, the estate minimum and bills land every 7 shifts. Miss it twice and you are on the street.

LOOP 03 · WEEKSThe ledger

Pay named creditors, or dispute the statement itself. Each clears into a scene and a choice that changes your numbers for good.

02 · Shift engine

One seed. One shift.

Shift.generate(seed, level, bump) is deterministic and offline: same inputs, same job, stations, puzzle kinds and floor. Everything scales with the shift's grade dl = max(0, floor + bump). The floor sets layout and rent; the bump comes from your player level and recent form (see Progression). Drag the sliders to see any point of a run.

Very easy / very hard shifts puzzle tier ∓1 and ghost count ∓1. Pay stays exactly ×100.

Gradedl = max(0, level + bump). level = floor − 1. Training ignores bump.
Rooms4 + 2·min(2, dl÷3) → 4 (dl 0-2), 6 (3-5), 8 (6+). Exit is locked until every terminal is solved.
Puzzle countmin(min(rooms−2,4), 1 + dl÷2 + rand(0..1)). Training: 1.
Tier 0-3(dl÷2 + skew).clamp(0,3). From tier 1 the five "twist" puzzles ask a second question (see Puzzles).
Ghosts1 + (rooms≥6) + (rooms≥8 && dl≥9) + streak÷3 + skew + audit + pair, clamped 1-4. A co-op pair gets one more.
Dark rooms0 on 4 rooms, 1 from 6, 2 on 8. Tighter light (×0.6) and the shift's finds are richer. Never in training.
Payout(normalPay·100 + 600·tasks − 40) × (100 + heat% + story%)/100, normalPay = wage + rand(0..4) + 2·dl. Heat is +10% per clean shift, max +50%.
Clockmax(150, 60 + 45·rooms − 6·dl) s × thermos 1.15 × calm 1.2 × filthy flat 0.95 × (1 − 0.2·hard) × (1 + story clock mod).
Hint price60 + 35·dl from tier 1 up; the 2nd hint costs double; vouchers first.
TrainingSeed 1337, office, one puzzle. Pays exactly $1,500 (940 + 600 − 40).
The boardThree different jobs. Each is the most novel of 12 candidate seeds against your last 30 shifts (job, setting, floor signature, puzzle kinds), cached so the board is cheap to read.
Floor plan2-column grid of 8×8-cell rooms. Six layout styles: tree, ring, snake, comb, ladder, loops; every floor is connected (tests prove it). A repair pass clears any room whose furniture cuts a station off.
Room templates24 furniture layouts (pillars, aisles, beds, bunks, pipes, checker…), each proven to keep the room connected. Settings lean to their own (hospital: beds, curtains, tables, islands). A floor uses each once before repeating.
VerbsDrag to walk. The action button picks the nearest of task / supply / locker / exit; a locker wins when a hunter is within 200 px or chasing. TOSS throws along your facing (or the longest clear line); it says why when it can't. Movement leaks a noise ping every ~6s.
RoundsSettings → View: true first person (eyes, one arm, a torch). A panorama per room, hunters close in over ~8s, torch slows them, battery + crank. Dark rooms are not yet handled there.

Levels, stars, a tower

Three layers of progress stack on top of the money: a player level that lifts the difficulty, a three-star review on every shift, and the Building, a 100-floor tower that is the same for everyone.

04 · Hunters

What is looking for you

One base hunter per job setting, but every shift assembles its own from that base. Speed, sight and ear multiply the AI. The centre tick on each bar is 1.0, the baseline (these are the base values; the shift's hunter drifts ±8%).

AI by level · modes patrol / investigate / chase / search
LVL 0-2Wanders. Chases what she hears and turns toward loud noise.
LVL 3-5Walks a room-to-room patrol. After losing you she searches your last position and the rooms beside it (9 + 3·iq seconds).
LVL 6+Lurks where you still have to go, camps the exit once all tasks are done, checks lockers near where you vanished.
ALWAYS4s grace at start. One sighting calls the others once. Chase speed min(80, 48 + 4·danger), investigate 46, search 40, patrol 30 + 4·iq.
Locker encounter · state machine
closingwatchingapproachlistenleavingfoundopening

A ghost within ~70px walks to the slats and listens for 6 + 1.5·iq s (6, 7.5 or 9). The mic reads loudness only — nothing is recorded. Strike 1 is a warning, strike 2 is found (jumpscare, $0). You can only leave in watching.

No mic, a too-loud room, or Crowd mode → hold-to-breathe button. Breath is finite (≈10s) and gasping counts as a noise. Game audio ducks while hiding so it can't trip the mic.

Assembled hunters

assemble(setting, seed) is deterministic, so both phones in a pair and a replayed audit meet the same hunter. It takes the setting's body, a face donated by another kind, a turned palette (8 hues), drifted stats and height, an origin line, and one rule. The contract shows only the rule's vague tell.

RuleContract tellWhat it really does
None—No quirks.
Still"It watches for movement."Stand still and it loses you: sight ×0.35 while you aren't moving.
Bold"It gets worse as the night goes on."Speed ramps from 80% to 125% across the shift. Do the dangerous rooms first.
Loud"It hears everything."Ear ×1.5, sight ×0.75. Quiet beats distance.

The Field Guide (Ledger → Guide) counts shifts survived per hunter key: at 1 it reveals who it was, at 2 the Agency's line, at 3 the full rule.

Weapons

Bought in Shop → ARMS, equipped at once. Carrying one replaces TOSS with an attack button. Training is unarmed. Hunters have hunterHp = 3. A hit staggers the hunter (it can't move or catch you), enough hits take it off the floor until it respawns far from you ("IT IS BACK"). A fight is loud and other hunters come to look. Hits need a clear line.

06 · Puzzles

Eighteen kinds, fifty-seven variants

Each puzzle is a pure function of (seed, tier, skin), and skin = (seed÷5) mod skins. From tier 1, five kinds add a twist (a second question laid over the clue), chosen by the seed. The widget and its hints read the same spec, so a hint can never lie. Pull the full answer for any puzzle with hintsFor(kind, seed, tier, skin).last.

08 · Home & flat

The flat is part of the game

The bedroom is first person: you come in facing the door. Five panels (yaw −2…2): living room (Mum, Dad's urn on his chair, his photo) · phone · door · desk · kitchen (Mae, the small one). The phone and bills are corner icons that carry over to the town and street. The family move one step each time you look away from a family room, capped by how eerie the flat is.

09 · The story

Every debt is a person

Eight debts in five chapters, plus the statement itself (see Debt mystery). Pay in instalments: at 50% you hear their side, at 100% the end scene plays and you must pick one of two accounts of the truth. Choices set flags that later scenes read, so the cases lean on each other. After a chapter's last debt, an Audit shift opens the next one.

10 · The debt mystery

Whose debt is it?

The $60,000 estate is six itemised lines (they sum to exactly Run.startEstate). Each line has a hidden truth drawn per save (saveSeed): who took it, what for, who got the money, and why it landed on you. You gather clues, then dispute a line by naming all three slots. Only a whole correct set strikes it; a wrong guess tells you nothing about which slot was wrong.

11 · Two-player

Runner & Operator

Two phones, one floor. The Runner plays the shift; the Operator sits at a night desk with a live map. Sessions run over Firestore; both phones rebuild the same shift from (seed, level, jobIndex, heat, hard, bump). A pair gets one more hunter and a slightly shorter clock. Pay splits 60 / 40 (Operator 40%).

What the Operator can do · OpsRules
LockLock a door for 8 s · cooldown 20 s · max 5. A lock never pins the Runner.
BellRing a bell to lure hunters · cooldown 10 s · max 6.
LightsKill a room's lights for 10 s · cooldown 25 s · max 3 (hunters hear ×0.4 there).
PingMark a room · 2 s cooldown.
Blackout & manualThe Operator holds the manual; its second page opens after a puzzle has been open 20 s. Runner hints are replaced by "Your operator has the manual."
Sealed terminals & the map
Override codeA co-op terminal is sealed until a 4-digit override code (shown only on the Operator's screen) is keyed. A wrong code makes noise (0.6) and locks the keypad for 10 s: guessing is not free.
Live mapThe Runner sends position, facing, hidden and every hunter (chasing / down) about 4×/s on live/pos. OperatorMap draws the whole plan, eases each thing along its last velocity, shows a fading trail, locked and dark rooms, and a FOLLOW zoom. Cameras are gone (the Operator sees everything).
Edge casesNo runner rejoin mid-shift; the operator session closes; disconnect and rejoin handled; the lobby is quiet (no knocks, phone or home music while any multiplayer screen is open).
12 · Door visitors

Someone is knocking

The hidden second system. Between shifts, someone knocks. The game secretly rolls what they really are and never tells you. You open the door and give 5%, 12% or 25% of the cycle's rent — or nothing. Weights: base human 4 / ghost 4 / test 2, × the visitor's bias, nudged by earlier flags.

Human

  • Gave: +1 voucher
  • Refused: −1 voucher; a note, "you were the last door they tried"

Ghost

  • Gave: haunted — hunters +4% for 2 shifts, money gone
  • Refused: nothing happens. That's the worst of it

Test

  • Gave: pay +4% for 3 shifts
  • Refused: pay +6% for 3 shifts. Refusing pays more.
