FamFi — on supremolabs.com

Juwa Wealth becomes FamFi, short for Family Finance. There are two surfaces. /famfi is the release page in FamFi's own wallet world. /famfi/personal is a desktop console with every control the phone app has, laid out for long money sessions.

2 · /famfi/personal — the console

This is a working mockup: click the tabs, click a card, and type into the cheque. The wallet stays open on the left on every tab, the way the phone opens on Accounts, so every edit shows on the cards straight away. On Payday each card shows the amount it's about to receive. Keyboard: 1–4 switch tabs, N adds a new entry, ⌘↵ saves.

Can we afford it?

From Josh Chequingunfold_more
Why the wallet stays putOn the phone the wallet is its own tab, so you check a balance and then go do the task. On a desktop there's room to keep it open next to every task and see each change land on the cards.
What changes for desktopTables replace the phone's one-at-a-time lists, a calendar replaces the Bills scroll, and hover actions replace swipes. The data, the payday math and the notifications are the same ones the app computes.
Below 900px wideThe wallet folds into a strip across the top and each tab stacks into one column. It still works on a laptop half-screen, but on a phone the app is the better tool.

3 · Sign-in gate

Opening /famfi/personal while signed out shows this screen. It uses the same Sign in with Apple the phones will use, and only the two household members get past it. Nothing loads before sign-in, not even the account names.
