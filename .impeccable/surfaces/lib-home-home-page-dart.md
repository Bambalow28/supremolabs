# Home (`/`)

Scope: supremolabs.com root. Mode: Experience (studio portfolio). Audience: app users arriving from one product, and credibility checkers. Job: see the whole lineup as one studio's body of work, open a shipped product. Proof: real app screens where they exist (TravelSync, WealthSync, WorkIt); typographic sleeves elsewhere, never fake screens. Founder note with a labeled portrait placeholder until the user supplies a photo.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Supremo Labs is a record label and every app is a catalogued release. Refuses the Dribbble default of a giant name over a bento grid of screenshots.

OWN-WORLD: matte near-black board; square sleeves flooded in each product's own color with its real screen rising from the sleeve's bottom edge; forthcoming releases as blank keyline sleeves with a FORTHCOMING stamp; catalog numbers SL 001–SL 011 as the only numbering; Big Shoulders Display (condensed, label-masthead weight) over Hanken Grotesk.

STORY: visitor reads the label name, flips through the crate, pulls a sleeve face-out, opens that release; the discography confirms the full catalog and what is out now; liner notes say one person makes all of it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: SUPREMO LABS masthead filling the measure edge to edge, one line under it counting out-now vs forthcoming from data; below, the crate: every release as a spine in catalog order with one sleeve pulled face-out at ~460px, its title, tagline and Open action beside the shelf's bottom edge.

FORM: Record Label Catalog, Impeccable's pick (rank 1 of 7), chosen over the rolled Poster Series. Seed key 4978c52a. Signature interaction: crate-dig, a spine widens into its sleeve as the previous one closes back to a spine.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
